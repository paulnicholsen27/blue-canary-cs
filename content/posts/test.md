\# How We Transformed District Scholars into a Fast, Accessible, High-Performing Website



If you have ever clicked on a website, waited three seconds, sighed, and hit the back button, you already understand why website speed matters.



That was exactly the problem District Scholars was facing before their redesign.



Their website was slow, outdated, difficult to use on mobile devices, and missing key accessibility features. It was hurting their search rankings, frustrating visitors, and limiting growth.



So we set out to change that.



At \*\*\[Blue Canary Web Design](/)\*\*, we specialize in building fast, accessible, and SEO-friendly websites for small businesses. This project is a great example of what happens when performance and usability come first.



\---



\## The Challenge: A Website That Was Holding Them Back



Before the redesign, DistrictScholars.com struggled with several issues:



\- Slow page load times  

\- Poor Google PageSpeed scores  

\- Limited mobile responsiveness  

\- Accessibility gaps  

\- Outdated layout and navigation  

\- Unoptimized images and scripts  



These problems affected both user experience and search visibility.



In today’s competitive online landscape, a slow website is more than an inconvenience. It is a lost opportunity.



\---



\## Our Approach: Rebuild, Optimize, and Future-Proof



Rather than applying temporary fixes, we took a full performance-first approach.



Our process included:



\- Technical performance audits  

\- Modern front-end rebuild  

\- Image and asset optimization  

\- Responsive layout redesign  

\- Accessibility improvements  

\- SEO-friendly structure and metadata  

\- Code cleanup and security hardening  



Every decision focused on long-term stability, speed, and ease of maintenance.



Learn more about our approach on our \*\*\[Services page](/services/)\*\*.
