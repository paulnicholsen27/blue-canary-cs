## Case Study: How Blue Canary Web Design Transformed Within Reach Therapy

When the team at **[Within Reach Therapy](www.withinreachtherapy.com)** approached us, their website had an important job: help people who are struggling find support quickly and comfortably. The practice offers services including anxiety therapy, trauma therapy, depression treatment, LGBTQ+ affirming therapy, and financial therapy, all delivered through online sessions for clients across multiple states. 

But their website... had some issues.

The original Squarespace site worked, but it suffered from several common problems we see with DIY builds: heavy images, inconsistent layout spacing, unclear hierarchy, and slow load performance. In mental health especially, first impressions matter. Research shows visitors form a judgment about a site’s credibility almost instantly, and slow loading can cause people to leave before they even see what the practice offers.

Our goal at **Blue Canary Web Design** was simple: redesign the site so it felt calm, trustworthy, and fast.

### Step 1: Simplifying the Design

Therapy websites should reduce stress, not create it. We restructured the layout so visitors immediately understand three things:

1. What the practice does
2. Who it helps
3. How to get started

The redesigned homepage now leads with a clear message about therapy for a “rich and meaningful life,” followed by direct paths to key services and an easy way to book a free consultation.

We also simplified navigation, reduced visual clutter, and created a clearer hierarchy with stronger headings and better spacing. The goal was to make the site feel welcoming rather than overwhelming.

### Step 2: Improving Performance

One of the biggest technical upgrades was speed optimization.

Many Squarespace sites suffer from oversized images and unnecessary scripts. We rebuilt key sections of the site, compressed media, and streamlined page elements so the browser has far less work to do when loading the page.

This matters because users often abandon sites that take more than three seconds to load. ([Fazal Designs](https://fazal.site/therapist-website-design-a-complete-guide-to-attracting-more-clients?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

After optimization, the site achieved a **100 PageSpeed score**, dramatically improving performance and user experience.

![page speed score showing 100% in performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO](/assets/images/uploads/withinreach_pagespeed_after.png "100% baby!")

### Step 3: Designing for Conversion

A therapy website isn’t just a digital brochure. It’s a guide that helps someone take the first step toward getting help.

We made several changes designed to increase inquiries:

* Prominent “Free Consultation” calls to action
* Clear service sections for anxiety, depression, trauma, and LGBTQ+ therapy
* Stronger visual flow from introduction to contact
* Improved mobile responsiveness

These changes make it easier for visitors to move from learning about the practice to reaching out for help.

### Step 4: SEO-Friendly Structure

We also reorganized page structure and content to improve search visibility. Therapy websites benefit from dedicated service pages and clear keyword signals so potential clients can find the practice when searching for mental health support online. 

The updated site structure helps search engines understand the services offered while keeping the writing natural and approachable for readers.

### The Result

The redesigned **Within Reach Therapy** website is faster, clearer, and more welcoming than the original version.

Instead of fighting through slow pages or confusing layouts, visitors now experience a site that loads instantly and guides them naturally toward booking a consultation.

For a therapy practice, that matters. The easier it is for someone to reach out, the more likely they are to take that first step toward getting help.
